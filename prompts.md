## 2. Prompt 1: UGC Advertisement Script

### Objective
Generate a relatable short-form video advertisement.

### Prompt

Act as a professional UGC advertising scriptwriter.

Create a 30-second UGC-style video advertisement for
Bean & Bloom Café.

Product: Cold Coffee

Target Audience: Students and young professionals.

Brand Tone: Friendly, casual, and relatable.

Structure:
1. Attention-grabbing opening hook.
2. Introduce a relatable situation.
3. Present the cold coffee as the featured product.
4. Describe the experience.
5. End with a clear call to action.

Include:
- Scene descriptions.
- Dialogue.
- On-screen text.
- A closing CTA.

Avoid unsupported claims about the product.

## 4. Prompt 3: Problem-Solution-CTA Advertisement

### Objective
Create an advertisement using a problem-solution-CTA structure.

### Prompt

Act as a social media advertising specialist.

Create a short advertisement for Bean & Bloom Café's cold coffee.

Use the following structure:

1. Problem: Introduce a relatable everyday situation.
2. Solution: Present the cold coffee as a refreshing option.
3. CTA: Encourage the audience to explore the café's menu.

Tone: Friendly, warm, and conversational.

Include a headline, short ad copy, and CTA.
