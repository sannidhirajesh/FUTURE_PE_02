# AI Content Marketing Using UGC Ads
## Bean & Bloom Café

---

## 1. Project Overview

This project uses prompt engineering to create engaging
advertising content for Bean & Bloom Café, a fictional
coffee shop.

The focus is on creating relatable UGC-style advertisements
for cold coffee and adapting the content for social media.

---

## 2. Product Information

- Business Name: Bean & Bloom Café
- Product: Cold Coffee
- Target Audience: Students and young professionals
- Brand Tone: Friendly, casual, and welcoming
- Tagline: Where Every Sip Feels Like Home.

---

## 3. UGC Advertisement Script

### Title: A Little Coffee Break

**Duration:** Approximately 30 seconds

**Scene 1 – The Hook (0–5 seconds)**

Visual: A student sitting at a desk with books and a laptop.

Dialogue:
"Ever feel like you need a little break from studying?"

On-screen text:
"Study break? ☕"

**Scene 2 – The Product (5–12 seconds)**

Visual: The student picks up a chilled cold coffee.

Dialogue:
"I decided to treat myself to something refreshing."

On-screen text:
"Meet your new coffee break."

**Scene 3 – The Experience (12–22 seconds)**

Visual: The student takes a sip and relaxes.

Dialogue:
"Honestly, this is exactly the kind of little moment
I needed today."

On-screen text:
"Pause. Sip. Relax."

**Scene 4 – The Call to Action (22–30 seconds)**

Visual: A close-up of the cold coffee with the café name.

Dialogue:
"If you're around, check out Bean & Bloom Café
and find your favorite sip!"

On-screen text:
"Visit Bean & Bloom Café ☕"

## 8. Conclusion

This project demonstrates how structured prompts can
generate advertising hooks, UGC-style scripts,
social media captions, and promotional content
for a local coffee shop.

different social media platforms.
